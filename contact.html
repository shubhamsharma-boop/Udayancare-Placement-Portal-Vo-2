FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before registering or hiring.

Who can register on UCPP?

The portal is primarily designed for Udayan Care Shalini Fellows.

Is registration free?

Yes. Registration and job applications are completely free.

How do employers hire candidates?

Employers register, complete verification and post job opportunities.

Will I receive interview updates?

Yes. Application status and interview updates appear on your dashboard.

Can employers search profiles?

Yes. Verified employers can search candidate profiles based on skills and job requirements.
