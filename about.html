About the Portal

Connecting Talent with Meaningful Opportunities

Udayan Care Placement Portal is designed to connect candidates with employment opportunities while enabling employers to discover suitable talent through a simple and accessible platform.

Udayan Care

Making Young Lives Shine

Udayan Care is a non-profit organisation committed to empowering children, young adults and women through education, mentoring, employability and other developmental initiatives.

The Placement Portal supports the employability journey by bringing candidates, employers and employment opportunities together on a structured digital platform.

Our Purpose

One Platform. Better Connections.

The portal simplifies how candidates discover opportunities and how employers connect with suitable candidates.

01

Opportunity Discovery

Candidates can explore available jobs and identify opportunities aligned with their qualifications, skills and career interests.

02

Employer Connect

Employers can engage with the platform to share employment opportunities and connect with relevant candidates.

03

Structured Placement Support

The platform supports candidate mapping, opportunity sharing, application tracking, interview coordination and placement processes.
